#Skybox Privacy Policy

## Information on your device

Skybox stores your observing sites, photography plans, camera/lens profiles, preferences and recoverable working draft on your device. You can use manual locations and the bundled astronomy, catalogs and calculators without an account or an internet connection. Skybox does not provide app cloud synchronization. Normal device backups may include saved content, depending on your device's backup settings.

Skybox has no advertising, analytics SDK, tracking identifier or user account. It does not upload plans, camera frames or your location to a Skybox backend.

## Camera, location and motion

Camera access is requested when you explicitly enable the AR sky view. Camera frames are used transiently on the device and are not saved or uploaded by Skybox. Camera use stops when that feature is no longer active.

Location access is requested when you choose “Use my location.” The location is used to position the sky and calculate local planning information. You can decline and choose coordinates manually. An optional “Improve location accuracy” action requests more precise location for the relevant task. Skybox does not request background location.

Device motion and heading can move the sky view while those features are active. Touch navigation remains available. You can manage camera and location access in iOS Settings.

## Network services

Apple Maps and place search contact Apple when you use those services; relevant map and search requests are subject to Apple's own privacy practices. Skybox does not download Apple basemaps for offline redistribution. An app-owned coordinate grid is available when a basemap is unavailable.

Optional satellite orbital data comes from CelesTrak. A request can occur when you choose Download, or when you use live Sky with the satellite layer enabled and the “When used” preference selected. Manual mode disables automatic acquisition. These requests retrieve a public orbital-data group and do not include your saved sites or plans. As with other HTTPS services, the provider receives the request and connection information. Provider restrictions can pause further requests.

There is no light-pollution data service in this first release.

## Retention and deletion

You can delete individual saved plans, sites and equipment profiles. Plans keep their copied site/equipment values when you delete a library entry. Reset Preferences, Clear Temporary Cache, Delete User Content and Erase Local Content have separate scopes described in the app. Removing a recoverable download stage also removes the ability to retry its installation offline.

Skybox retains minimal local provider request history across its reset actions so resets cannot bypass provider rate limits or error pauses. This contains request phase/status, deadlines and dataset revision/checksum references, not your location or plan content. Uninstalling the app is outside that reset mechanism.

Skybox cannot delete copies in device backups or files you exported to another app. If you explicitly share a bundled source file, iOS sends it to the destination you select; Skybox does not automatically share your content.
